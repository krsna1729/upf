Clauses: 5.5.3.3

5.5.3.3	QoS Flow Identifier (QFI)
Description: When present this parameter indicates the QoS Flow Identifier of the QoS flow to which the transferred packet belongs.
Value range: {0..26-1}. 
Field length: 6 bits.
